Background

Alliyah brings more than 20 years of tutoring and coaching experience, working with students of every ability — from those rebuilding their confidence to those aiming for the very highest grades.

Alongside teaching, she has built a career spanning investment banking, international programme management and community leadership. She is the founder of Feed a Friend, one of Qatar's largest grassroots volunteer movements, and her work has been recognised through Her Highness Sheikha Moza bint Nasser's Individual Contributions Award for the State of Qatar. She was also shortlisted in the Top 30 of the Inc. Arabia Women of Influence Awards, recognising women making a meaningful impact across the region.

Teaching philosophy

Students rarely struggle because they lack ability — more often, they simply haven't been shown the right tools or given the chance to use them with confidence. Once a student understands a concept, practises proven methods and sees those methods working, something changes: they stop second-guessing themselves and start believing in their own ability. That belief is the foundation of the A* Accelerator.

Every programme is built to develop knowledge and confidence together. Rather than teaching students what to memorise, Alliyah helps them understand the thinking behind Business and Economics, so they can approach unfamiliar questions with clarity, apply their knowledge effectively, and perform confidently under exam conditions.

Approach & resources

All learning resources, including the A* Playbooks, are created by Alliyah herself — built around examination requirements and brought to life with real-world business and economic examples. Her aim goes beyond exam results: to help students develop the skills, judgement and confidence that will serve them long after they leave the classroom.

"Confidence grows with every moment a student realises, 'This works. I can do this.'"
